# AI Engineering Lab

> Building AI systems beyond the demo.

Welcome to my personal AI Engineering knowledge base.

This repository is my second brain for everything related to modern Artificial Intelligence engineering. It is where I research, organize, connect, and refine ideas while designing production-grade AI systems.

The goal is not simply to collect notes, but to build a living knowledge graph that continuously evolves and serves as the foundation for articles, books, talks, courses, open-source projects, and real-world software.

---

## Philosophy

Technology changes quickly.

Concepts last much longer.

Instead of organizing knowledge around frameworks or trends, this vault focuses on understanding the fundamental principles behind AI systems, software architecture, distributed systems, and engineering practices.

Every new concept should connect with existing knowledge.

Everything should be reusable.

Everything should be versioned.

Everything should be documented.
